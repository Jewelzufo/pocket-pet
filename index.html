How to play

  1. Hatch the egg, give your pet a name, and keep its Food, Fun and Energy bars up with the pocket-device buttons.
  2. When all three bars are above 70 your pet is Thriving and every XP gain earns +25%.
  3. Play mini-games to earn XP and coins. Games cost 8 energy each. New games unlock at Lv 5, 12, 20, 32 and 45.
  4. Growth follows the same road: a new form roughly every 4–5 levels, milestone forms on the unlock levels, and the golden Adult form at Lv 99.
  5. Check in daily to build your streak (you get one grace day), finish the three daily quests, and spend coins in the Shop.
  6. Items come in four rarities — Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Gear, shards and boosts are only ever earned through play; cosmetics are the exception — the Shop's Cosmetics section sells 110 of those for coins, and every level-up unlocks a random one you don't own yet. Your pet card displays a Collector Tier computed from your collection.
  7. Your pet auto-saves on this device and earns a little XP while you're away. Download save / Import save in the Save tab moves it anywhere.
